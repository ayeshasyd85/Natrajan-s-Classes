Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? You'll probably find the answer here.

Which boards do you cover?

We teach all major boards — CBSE, ICSE, and State Board — with separate batches where needed so every child studies their exact syllabus.

What are the batch timings?

Morning batches for school-goers (7–10 AM) and evening batches (4–8 PM). Weekend batches are also available for working students. Exact timings are shared at admission.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the class and subjects chosen. Primary classes start at an affordable monthly rate, with a sibling discount of 10%. Contact us on WhatsApp for the full fee structure.

Do you provide study material?

Yes! Printed notes, worksheets, and previous-year question papers are included in the fees — no extra charges.

Is there a free trial class?

Absolutely. We offer 2 free demo classes before you decide. Book one using the form below or on WhatsApp.

How do parents track progress?

Every month you receive a report card with test scores and teacher remarks. We also run a WhatsApp group for each batch with attendance and announcements.
